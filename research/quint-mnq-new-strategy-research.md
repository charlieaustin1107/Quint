# Quint MNQ: Five new strategy research candidates

21 November 2026 · Literature review and repository audit · No backtests run

The best next experiment is not another opening-range, VWAP, or gap variant. Start with a **VIX futures curve recovery screen**, using daily data to ask whether a change in market-wide stress improves on a plain MNQ rebound rule. Separately, two Fed studies, a weekly auction setup, and an order-book refill test offer distinct research paths. None has an independently reproduced, after-cost MNQ edge in this review.

## What counts as new to Quint

I screened tracked strategy source, notes, the Lab registry, and 42 available branch tips, including the open strategy research PRs, against [`main` at `c5a6de7`](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/commit/c5a6de7f706ed785c234274367b8cb71d869176b). “New” means I did not find the same decision input and trigger specified in the material reviewed. It does not mean the concept is new to trading or absent from private notes or unpublished work.

- **Already covered:** Opening-range and candle breakouts, VWAP trend/fade and first pullback, gap fade/midpoint, noise-area and late-day momentum, RSI pullbacks, failed prior-day breakouts, and the Five Pillars playbook ([Bot Lab](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/botlab/README.md), [Lab registry](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/lab/brain/registry.json), [Quint plan](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/PLAN.md)).
- **Already in research:** Best-bid/ask imbalance plus microprice continuation, the 55-session Donchian breakout, monthly time-series momentum with a valuation/yield-curve filter, and a contract-roll test ([OFI note](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/research/mnq/notes/2026-11-19-scalping-ofi-microprice.md), [PR #24](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/24), [PR #31](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/31), [PR #29](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/29)).
- **Not recycled:** The plan already considers and rejects the classic turn-of-the-month trade and pre-Fed drift. Etula et al. add useful historical context about institutional cash needs and settlement-driven reversals, but their primary U.S. equity sample used an earlier settlement regime, and the paper does not establish a modern MNQ trade ([Quint plan](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/PLAN.md), [Review of Financial Studies](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/1/75/5494694)).

## Shortlist, in recommended test order

| Candidate | What Quint would measure | Evidence and transfer risk | First useful test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VIX curve recovery** | The VIX futures curve goes from inverted to upward-sloping. Test whether this adds information to an MNQ recovery signal. | S&P 500 evidence, not MNQ; the proposed trigger is a different rule from the paper ([Fassas and Hourvouliades](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/12/3/113/pdf), [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/inside-volatility-trading-is-vix-backwardation-necessarily-a-sign-of-a-future-down-market/)). | Daily screen, then a roll-aware execution test only if it adds value. |
| **Post-FOMC swing reversal** | After the meeting and the next three full sessions, test whether the cumulative move reverses over the following week. | S&P 500 working-paper evidence at the relevant horizon; sparse events and uncertain Nasdaq transfer ([Boguth et al.](https://files.osf.io/v1/resources/zurfk_v1/providers/osfstorage/62a74fca2631491f71a3175f?action=download&direct&version=3)). | Daily event study using official dates; check if there are enough independent meetings. |
| **Fed announcement with Treasury confirmation** | Compare MNQ and 2-year Treasury futures immediately after the statement. Treat the press conference as a separate event. | Strong evidence that the two communications windows differ; no evidence here that the proposed short-window rule earns money ([SF Fed](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/working-papers/2025/12/financial-market-effects-of-fomc-communication-evidence-from-a-new-event-study-database/), [International Journal of Central Banking](https://www.ijcb.org/sites/default/files/ijcb-vn-market-impact-fed-communications-role-press-conference.pdf)). | Historical event study, then synchronized quote replay only if warranted. |
| **Weekly value-area rejection** | A break outside last week’s volume-based value area fails, price retests the boundary, then returns toward the highest-volume price. | 2026 preprint with a positive ES result but inconclusive pooled replication including NQ; no verified NQ-only after-cost result ([Perry preprint](https://www.ssrn.com/abstract=6728359)). | Reproduce the published ES result before adapting it to NQ or MNQ. |
| **Post-sweep order-book refill** | After an aggressive order consumes several prices, test whether rapid replenishment predicts a reversal or helps avoid poor fills. | E-mini research documents replenishment, not an MNQ trading edge; directional evidence from a separate study uses Chinese equities ([Journal of Futures Markets](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.22259), [order-book study](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.00731v2)). | Quote-based diagnostic first. If the move is gone by executable entry, stop or treat it only as a possible execution filter. |

## Test designs

The windows below are starting specifications to freeze before seeing results, not optimized settings or live trading instructions. For each idea, estimate how frequently the trigger occurs before spending time on a full backtest.

### VIX curve recovery: cheapest first screen

Fassas and Hourvouliades find evidence that the shape of the VIX futures curve may help forecast S&P 500 returns; their historical study fitted a slope using spot VIX and the nearest seven futures on 2010–2017 data ([paper and methods](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/12/3/113/pdf)). Cboe cautions that inversion is not a reliable instruction to short equities and that recoveries can be quick ([Cboe analysis](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/inside-volatility-trading-is-vix-backwardation-necessarily-a-sign-of-a-future-down-market/)). This is a different mechanism from Quint’s price-channel breakout and monthly valuation/yield-curve idea ([PR #24](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/24), [PR #31](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/31)).

- **First test:** From same-day standard monthly VX futures settlements, interpolate 30- and 60-calendar-day values only when listed expiries bracket each target. Use the 60-day value minus the 30-day value. Flag the first positive close after at least two consecutive non-positive closes; use only information published before entry. Screen a long-only MNQ recovery over the next five sessions; do not treat this simplified trigger as a replication of the paper.
- **Comparison:** Match non-signal sessions by prior MNQ drawdown and realized volatility so the result is not just “stocks bounce after a sell-off.” Also report same-duration unconditional long exposure and results with the largest stress episode removed.
- **Stop if:** Too few independent inversion episodes occur, results depend on one crisis, or the curve adds no benefit over matched MNQ-only rebounds. Start with [Cboe’s daily settlement tables](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/futures/market-statistics/settlement/futures/daily) and [historical-data catalogue](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/futures/market-statistics/historical-data/futures), then verify coverage, settlement fields and publication times before trusting the screen.

### Post-FOMC swing reversal: test the delayed move, not the headlines

Boguth et al. report that the FOMC-day move alone had little relationship with the next three days in their sample. The stronger result was a reversal after the cumulative announcement-through-day-three move, over subsequent windows such as days four through ten ([working paper](https://files.osf.io/v1/resources/zurfk_v1/providers/osfstorage/62a74fca2631491f71a3175f?action=download&direct&version=3)). That is a separate hypothesis from the pre-announcement drift already rejected by Quint, and from the short intraday event study below ([Quint plan](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/PLAN.md)).

- **First test:** Label scheduled meeting announcement day zero using the [official Federal Reserve calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm). From the prior session’s close through day three’s close, measure the cumulative move. At the next executable session open, screen the opposite direction through the close of day ten. Exclude unscheduled meetings from the primary specification; keep roll mapping and event timestamps point-in-time.
- **Comparison:** First reproduce the published S&P 500 relationship separately. On NQ and MNQ, compare with matched non-FOMC midweek windows, same-direction momentum and exposure-matched unconditional long results. Keep NQ proxy evidence and MNQ-native results in separate columns. Use meetings, not bars, as the independent units.
- **Stop if:** Direction or magnitude shifts materially between Fed regimes, the test is driven by a few dates, or the latest untouched sample cannot resolve the effect. This holds overnight, so treat it as research or alerts unless the precise account rules allow the hold.

### Fed statement with Treasury confirmation: a separate intraday experiment

The SF Fed’s working paper, revised in August 2026, reports that press conferences have become a more important source of policy news than statements for most assets. A separate study finds statement and press-conference responses can behave differently across Fed chairs ([SF Fed working paper](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/working-papers/2025/12/financial-market-effects-of-fomc-communication-evidence-from-a-new-event-study-database/), [International Journal of Central Banking](https://www.ijcb.org/sites/default/files/ijcb-vn-market-impact-fed-communications-role-press-conference.pdf)). Quint already acknowledges high-impact news and pre-Fed drift; I did not find a specified cross-market confirmation rule around the statement ([Quint plan](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/PLAN.md)).

- **First test:** On scheduled statement days with verified release and press-conference times, measure MNQ and 2-year Treasury futures (ZT) price moves from the statement release through 20 minutes after it. In the primary screen, compare continued MNQ direction after that decision time on same-sign versus opposite-sign MNQ/ZT moves, exiting the observation before the press conference starts. Use the price of ZT, not yield, when defining the sign. Evaluate any separate press-conference condition independently; never use the completed conference’s reaction to trade earlier within it.
- **Comparison:** Compare with MNQ statement momentum without ZT confirmation, a matched non-FOMC time-of-day placebo, and a side-preserving label shuffle by meeting. The SF Fed’s database is useful for event labels and its defined window changes, not a substitute for synchronized MNQ/ZT quotes at these custom timestamps ([USMPD methods](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2025-30.pdf)).
- **Stop if:** Confirmation fails to beat the price-only baseline, the remaining window is too short after realistic spread/slippage, or a few meetings explain the effect. This experiment does not override Quint’s news blocks. If the account excludes the window, keep any result as research or an informational alert only ([Quint plan](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/PLAN.md)).

### Weekly value-area rejection: a volume-based alternative to prior-day extremes

The existing failed-breakout bot uses yesterday’s high or low and a fixed reward-to-risk target ([Bot Lab](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/botlab/README.md)). The proposed alternative uses last week’s volume by traded price. A 2026 preprint reports a positive ES result, but pooled cross-market replication including NQ failed to reject the joint null at conventional levels ([SSRN preprint](https://www.ssrn.com/abstract=6728359)). The public abstract does not isolate NQ’s after-cost performance or link the claimed public replication code directly; verify the materials before treating the ES result as reproducible ([SSRN abstract](https://www.ssrn.com/abstract=6728359)).

- **First test:** Reproduce the source specification on ES before inventing an MNQ version. For the separate MNQ adaptation, freeze the previous completed regular-hours week’s volume-by-price distribution; define the value-area algorithm and tie-breaks in advance. Test an excursion outside, return inside and retest as three ordered events, targeting the prior week’s point of control, its most-traded price. Do not count a bar that makes all three without reliable tick ordering.
- **Comparison:** Use prior-week price extremes with the same entry confirmation and holding horizon, then match exit rules in a separate comparison to isolate whether volume-based levels are adding information. Use actual traded price and size; NinjaTrader’s minute-resolution profile distributes volume evenly across prices in the bar and is only an approximation ([NinjaTrader volume-profile guide](https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/order_flow_volume_profile.htm)).
- **Stop if:** ES cannot be reproduced, MNQ’s volume-defined levels do not beat simple price levels, or results depend on favorable same-bar ordering, holiday weeks or rolling between contracts. Do not silently splice the volume profile of two different expiries.

### Post-sweep order-book refill: test the fill before testing the scalp

Fishe, Haynes and Onur document how E-mini displayed liquidity replenishes and how timing varies with market conditions. A separate Chinese-equities study reports different price responses depending on how aggressively the original order walked the book; that does not establish what an MNQ sweep predicts ([E-mini resiliency](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.22259), [effective-market-order study](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.00731v2)). Quint’s existing research follows a five-second top-of-book imbalance and microprice skew; this candidate would wait for a shock and then measure replenishment on the swept side ([Quint OFI note](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/research/mnq/notes/2026-11-19-scalping-ofi-microprice.md)).

- **First test:** Reconstruct sequenced trades and multi-level depth for an actual MNQ expiry. Mark aggressive activity that consumes at least two price levels. At one second after the event, classify whether the spread has normalized and displayed liquidity on the depleted side has recovered versus the pre-event reference. Compare both a reversal hypothesis and an execution-quality use. Write down how clustered prints and ambiguous aggression are handled before studying labels.
- **Comparison:** Measure fixed-horizon returns after the one-second decision time against matched sweeps without refill and Quint’s existing OFI/microprice feature. Then mark executable prices at actual bid/ask plus modeled latency, slippage and fees. If testing passive orders, require defensible queue and partial-fill assumptions; displayed refill alone does not establish fillability ([CME Group liquidity analysis](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2025/reassessing-liquidity-beyond-order-book-depth.html)).
- **Stop if:** No incremental information survives the existing OFI baseline, the apparent effect is over before a plausible fill, or the data cannot reconstruct the event. Do not test this from candles or use it to route orders.

## What to do first, and how to call a result

- **Check data before optimizing.** The checked-in [Lab one-minute CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/c5a6de7f706ed785c234274367b8cb71d869176b/lab/data/mnq_1m.csv) contained 2,000 rows from 18–19 November 2026; the [daily CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/c5a6de7f706ed785c234274367b8cb71d869176b/lab/data/mnq_1d.csv) contained 1,899 rows with saved timestamps from May 2019 to November 2026. These are file counts, not an audit of trading sessions, missing data or per-expiry rolls. Start with the daily VIX or delayed-FOMC screens, then verify raw expiries and executable data before modelling returns as tradable ([roll-integrity PR #29](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/pull/29)).
- **Pre-register one primary version per candidate.** Freeze decision timestamp, universe, controls, fills, verified round-trip costs, adverse-cost checks, primary metric and failure rule. Perform chronological walk-forward checks, leave the latest genuinely unexamined block untouched, and report uncertainty, exposure, concentration and net expectancy against the relevant baseline. Cluster by meeting, stress episode, week or session as appropriate; don’t inflate the sample by counting related bars as independent ([Quint’s Backtest Integrity Standard](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/research/BACKTEST-INTEGRITY-STANDARD.md)).
- **Treat direct MNQ negative evidence seriously.** Mesfin’s 2026 preprints report that the commonly tested OHLCV signals and directional strategies built around a volatility-volume-gap classifier failed their stated cost and stability gates. The classifier may still be worth evaluating as a descriptive label once its first-30-minute input is available, but the papers do not justify adding it as another directional bot ([MNQ falsification study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004), [MNQ regime-classifier preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11423)).
- **Keep the run small.** Write the VIX candidate as one simulation-only ticket, join the data, count qualifying episodes and compare signal days with matched non-signal days before building a bot. If the sample is thin, record “inconclusive” rather than tuning thresholds until it looks good. The [Quint Control hold](https://github.com/charlieaustin1107/Quint/blob/main/quint-control/STATUS.md) and separate historical, paper and live gates remain unchanged.
